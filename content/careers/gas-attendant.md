## Part-Time Gas Attendant — Burns Auto Repair

**Location:** Newtown, PA (on-site)

**Type:** Part-time

**Start Date:** Mid-August (with a few training sessions beforehand)

**Great for:** High school and college students

**Hours:** Monday–Friday, 3:00 PM – 8:00 PM; Saturday 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Sunday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

### About Burns Auto Repair

Burns Auto Repair is a long-standing, family-owned auto repair shop serving the Newtown, PA community since 1957. We're known for honest diagnostics, dependable maintenance, and quality repair work across a wide range of vehicle needs. Our ASE-certified mechanics and friendly support team focus on transparent communication and safety for every customer. Team members work in a collaborative environment that values trust, professionalism, and community relationships.

### About the Role

We're hiring a few reliable, friendly Gas Attendants for the upcoming school year. This is a great part-time job for high school and college students looking for steady hours, hands-on experience, and a team that values dependability and good customer service.

As a full-service station, our attendants are the face of the shop at the pumps — greeting customers, fueling vehicles, and lending a hand wherever it's needed. You'll also help keep the shop running smoothly and handle closing-up duties at the end of the day.

### What You'll Do

- Attend the gas pumps at our full-service station — fueling vehicles, cleaning windshields, and helping customers add air to their tires
- Answer phone calls and schedule appointments
- Check out customers when they pick up their vehicles
- Accept vehicles for service and confirm we have the correct paperwork for inspections
- Restock the fridge with water and keep the oil rack stocked
- Clear trash cans, sweep the shop, and handle other closing-up duties
- Lock up at the end of the day

### What We're Looking For

- Friendly, dependable, and punctual
- Strong customer service skills and a positive attitude
- Willingness to work outdoors at the pumps in varying weather
- Able to work part-time hours during the school year, starting mid-August
- No prior experience needed — we'll train you

### How to Apply

Send a short note about yourself and your availability to **office@burnsautorepair.com**. We look forward to hearing from you!
