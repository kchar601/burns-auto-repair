## About the role

Burns' Auto Repair is looking for an experienced automotive technician to join our team. We are a family-owned shop that has served Newtown since 1957.

## What you'll do

- Perform PA state inspections and emissions testing
- Diagnose and repair brakes, steering, suspension, and electrical systems
- Complete scheduled maintenance services
- Keep clear notes on work performed

## What we're looking for

- 2+ years of professional automotive repair experience
- PA state inspection and emissions licenses (or willingness to obtain)
- Valid driver's license
- A team-first attitude and pride in quality work

## What we offer

- Competitive pay based on experience
- Steady work with an established local customer base
- A respectful, family-run shop environment
